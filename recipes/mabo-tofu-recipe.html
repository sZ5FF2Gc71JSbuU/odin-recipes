Mabo Tofu Recipe

Description

Mabo Tofu (麻婆豆腐, also spelled Mapo Tofu) is a popular Chinese-inspired dish in Japan. It features tofu simmered in a garlicky, spicy meat sauce and is typically served with a bowl of steamed rice. Although it originated in China, Mabo Tofu has become a beloved part of everyday Japanese home cooking. Our version is easy to make with ingredients you may already have in your Japanese pantry.

What's Mabo Tofu?

Mabo Tofu is so popular in Japan that you can find it on the menus of Chinese restaurants throughout the country. It's also a staple dinner entrée in many Japanese homes. Some people make the sauce from scratch, while others use instant sauce packets from the supermarket. With a packet of instant sauce, all you need to add is tofu. These convenient packets save time and eliminate the need to keep specialty Chinese seasonings in your kitchen that you might not use very often. The flavor is surprisingly good, too.

So why make Mabo Tofu from scratch? Because you can make a delicious Japanese-style version with ingredients from a typical Japanese pantry. Many people don't realize how easy it is.

Traditional Chinese Mabo Tofu typically uses fermented bean pastes, such as doubanjiang (a spicy fermented bean paste). In our recipe, we use miso as a substitute for some of these seasonings. You can use red miso or regular miso, depending on what you have on hand. For heat, we use Takanotsume, small dried Japanese chili peppers, instead of chili paste.

Keep in mind that this is not an authentic Chinese Mabo Tofu recipe, nor is it quite the same as the version you might find at a Chinese restaurant in the US or China. This is Japanese-style Mabo Tofu, a home-cooked version that has evolved to suit Japanese tastes, much like ramen and gyoza have become part of Japan's own food culture.

If you've never tried Japanese-style Mabo Tofu, give this easy recipe a try. It's quick, simple, and delicious.

Ingredients

  • 1 Tofu (about 14 oz or 400g)
  • 3 green onions
  • 1 Tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp garlic (minced)
  • 1 tsp ginger (minced)
  • 1 Japanese dried red pepper (Takanotsume)
  • 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Seasonings

  • 2 Tbsp Miso paste
  • 2 Tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 2 Tbsp Sake
  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 1 Tbsp Katakuriko potato starch (or corn starch)
  • 1 cup water

Steps

  1. Cut Tofu into 1" cubes. Slice green onions thinly. Set aside.
  2. In a bowl, mix all the ingredients for the seasonings until Miso dissolves. Set aside.
  3. Heat oil in a wok at medium heat, add garlic, ginger, and red pepper, and stir.
  4. Add ground pork and cook until browned.
  5. Add the seasonings and let it boil.
  6. Add cut Tofu and stir gently. When Tofu is cooked through, stir in green onions and sesame oil.
